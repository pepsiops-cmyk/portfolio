About

Whether it's a feature on the roadmap or friction nobody has flagged yet, the starting point is the same: what the user is actually trying to do. Their intent, the assumptions they bring, the moments they hesitate. The design comes from removing the friction and pain points in their way.

Most recently I was the sole designer on a consumer AI chat platform, owning UX end to end alongside a PM and the CEO. Before that: three years at an influencer marketing startup, designing tools for creators and brands across web and mobile, including the design system in Figma.

AI is part of the workflow: it means more ideas get prototyped and tested with real users before engineering builds anything.
