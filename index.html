About

Whether it's a feature on the roadmap or friction nobody has flagged yet, the starting point is the same: what the user is actually trying to do. Their intent, the assumptions they bring, the moments they hesitate. The design comes from removing the friction and pain points in their way.

2024–2026: sole designer on a consumer AI chat platform, owning UX end to end alongside a PM and the CEO.

2021–2024: three years at an influencer marketing platform, designing tools for creators and brands. That work included:

  • 4 tools for creators and brands, across web and native iOS and Android
  • A performance analytics dashboard (reach, impressions, engagement)
  • The platform's Figma design system and component library, shared by all 4 tools

AI is part of the workflow: it means more ideas get prototyped and tested with real users before engineering builds anything.
